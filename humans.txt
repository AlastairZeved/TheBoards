/* Site */
Purpose: Zeved Boards — a spatial, offline-first PWA. A fixed, bounded page
where any thought becomes a framed, movable, scalable note the instant it is
typed; structure is asserted after capture by where things sit and how big
they are. Built for neurodivergent users and spatial reasoners specifically: 
prioritizing working memory and a constant field of vision for relevant features
and information by avoiding screen switching, hidden buttons, and dense rows of 
buttons. A single bounded page where any thought becomes a framed, movable, 
scalable note the instant it's typed — capture precedes structure, always.
Form and function are intertwined at the seams, no decision is made in
isolation to prioritize coherence and user cognitive load.
Stack: Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS. No frameworks, no build step, no dependencies,
no backend. All data lives client-side in IndexedDB.
Design: The design system's authority is docs/UIUX.md; every interaction
ruling is numbered and recorded in docs/DECISIONS.md.
